Expense & Budget Tracker

View live project Source code

Overview

A responsive personal finance app for tracking income, managing expenses and keeping an eye on a monthly budget. It shows your balance, recent activity and spending patterns on one dashboard, and works the same on a phone as on a desktop.

Features

  • DashboardMonthly balance, income and expenses at a glance.
  • TransactionsAdd, filter and delete entries, with CSV export.
  • BudgetsSet a monthly cap and get an alert as you near it.
  • AnalyticsCategory breakdown and month-by-month spending bars.
  • Light & dark themesFollows your system setting, or choose your own.
  • Works on any screenSidebar on desktop, bottom tab bar on phones.

Tools

  • React
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Vite
